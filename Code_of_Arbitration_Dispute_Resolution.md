# World Search Council (WSC) — Code of Arbitration & Dispute Resolution

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Official Draft  
**Governance Body:** World Search Council (WSC) Arbitration Board  
**Maintained by:** WSC Legal & Governance Working Group  

---

## Preamble

The **WSC Arbitration Board** serves as the independent judicial body of the World Search Council. Its purpose is to enforce compliance with the *Code of Search Ethics* and *Code of Operations*, resolve operational disputes between clients and certified members, and safeguard the technical integrity of the search ecosystem without resorting to costly, protracted court litigation.

Adherence to decisions issued by the Arbitration Board is a mandatory condition of WSC membership, agency certification, and directory listing.

---

## §1 Structure & Panel Composition

### 1.1 Independence & Neutrality
* The Arbitration Board operates independently from commercial sponsors, tool vendors, and founding partners.
* Arbitrators are elected by the WSC Assembly for fixed, staggered terms to maintain continuity and impartiality.

### 1.2 Panel Selection
* Each dispute is reviewed by a panel of **three (3) independent arbitrators**:
  1. One technical lead from the WSC Working Groups.
  2. One peer practitioner (certified agency representative or independent consultant).
  3. One designated governance officer acting as Panel Chair.

### 1.3 Conflict of Interest & Recusal
* Arbitrators must immediately disclose any financial, professional, or personal connections to the involved parties.
* Any arbitrator with a direct conflict of interest is automatically recused and replaced by an alternate panelist.

---

## §2 Scope of Disputes & Filing Procedures

### 2.1 Eligible Complaints
The Board accepts petitions regarding:
* **Ethics Violations:** Breaches of the *Code of Search Ethics* (e.g., traffic manipulation, cloaking, scaled programmatic abuse).
* **Operational & Exit Failures:** Non-compliance with the *WSC Transfer Protocol (WTP)* or withholding client digital assets/credentials.
* **Audit & Certification Disputes:** Appeals against technical audit scores, badge revocations, or directory status changes.

### 2.2 Petition Submission
* Complaints must be submitted formally via the WSC Governance Portal (`worldsearchcouncil.org/arbitration/file`).
* Petitions must include:
  1. Detailed statement of facts and specific Code sections allegedly breached.
  2. Documented evidence (e.g., audit logs, communication records, repository histories, WTP export files).
  3. Desired remedy or corrective action.

---

## §3 Investigation & Proceedings

### 3.1 Due Process & Notice
* Upon receiving a valid petition, the Board issues a formal **Notice of Dispute** to the respondent within 5 business days.
* The respondent has **14 calendar days** to submit a formal defense, counter-evidence, or remediation proposal.

### 3.2 Technical Verification
* The Board may delegate technical verification to the WSC Technical Working Group.
* Technical auditors inspect server logs, JSON-LD schemas, redirect structures, and WSC Hybrid Tracker data to establish objective factual grounds.

### 3.3 Confidentiality
* All evidence, technical logs, and internal deliberations remain strictly confidential during ongoing proceedings to protect proprietary business information.

---

## §4 Rulings & Sanctions

The Board evaluates evidence against WSC standards and issues a binding decision by majority vote.

### 4.1 Remediation Orders & Penalties
Depending on severity, the Board may impose the following actions:

* **Level 1 — Private Caution & Mandatory Remediation:**  
  Issued for minor or unintentional operational omissions. The party is given **14 days** to fix technical flaws or deliver missing WTP handoff items.

* **Level 2 — Temporary Suspension:**  
  Issued for severe operational neglect or failure to comply with Level 1 orders. Results in immediate suspension of WSC Certified status, badge removal, and hidden status in the public directory.

* **Level 3 — Permanent Revocation & Registry Marking:**  
  Issued for malicious violations (e.g., client asset holding, malicious site destruction, intentional metric fraud)[cite: 1, 2]. Results in permanent expulsion from the WSC and a public status update on the verification registry (`worldsearchcouncil.org/verify/[ID]`).

---

## §5 Appeals Process

1. Parties may appeal a Level 2 or Level 3 ruling within **14 calendar days** of issuance.
2. Appeals are granted only on the grounds of **procedural irregularity** or **new, previously unavailable technical evidence**.
3. Appeals are reviewed by an expanded 5-member panel of the WSC Assembly, whose verdict is final and unappealable within the WSC ecosystem.
