# World Search Council (WSC) — Code of Operations

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Official Draft  
**Governance Body:** World Search Council (WSC)  
**Maintained by:** WSC Technical & Operations Working Group  

---

## Preamble

The **Code of Operations** defines the mandatory operational standards, technical audit protocols, and data transfer requirements for all certified agencies, independent consultants, and enterprise partners within the World Search Council (WSC) ecosystem[cite: 1, 2]. While the *Code of Search Ethics* establishes ethical principles, this document governs operational execution, client handoff workflows, and technical compliance[cite: 1, 2].

---

## §1 Documentation & Asset Handoff Standards (WSC Transfer Protocol)

### 1.1 Anti-Lock-In Mandate
* Certified members and agencies are strictly prohibited from creating technical or operational vendor lock-in[cite: 1].
* Obfuscating site configurations, withholding credential access, or encrypting strategic documentation to restrict client autonomy is a direct breach of WSC operational standards.
* All domain properties, custom tracking schemas, server configurations, and search engine console rights remain the exclusive property of the client.

### 1.2 Mandatory Exit Package (WSC Transfer Protocol - WTP)
Upon termination or completion of an agency agreement, the practitioner must deliver a standardized **WTP Exit Package** within 14 calendar days[cite: 1]. The package must include:
* **Technical Architecture Logs:** Complete records of site structure modifications, active server-side redirects, canonical rules, and crawling directives (`robots.txt`, `/llms.txt`).
* **Schema & Entity Mappings:** Complete repository of custom JSON-LD structured data, Knowledge Graph references, and entity association maps.
* **Telemetry & Analytics Sovereignty:** Ownership transfer of all analytics properties, tag manager containers, and WSC Hybrid Tracker nodes.
* **Audit & Performance History:** All historical audit reports, benchmark datasets, and technical health score logs compiled during the engagement period.

## §2 Technical Auditing & Scoring Standards

### 2.1 The WSC Search & Health Scoring Protocol
To prevent synthetic metric gamification, all technical assessments and performance audits conducted under WSC certification must adhere to the **3-Phase WSC Scoring Standard**[cite: 4]:
* **Phase I: Technical Readiness (0–50 Points):** Evaluates foundational technical health, server infrastructure, schema validity, SSL/TLS configuration, and core performance standards[cite: 4].
* **Phase II: Crawl & AI Throughput (51–70 Points):** Measures bot-ingestion efficiency, Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) readiness, RAG crawler visibility, and server-side tracking health via the WSC Hybrid Tracker[cite: 4, 5].
* **Phase III: Market Success & Engagement (71–100 Points):** Validates real-world user interaction, temporal stability, and organic search efficacy[cite: 4].

### 2.2 Server-Side Tracking & Telemetry Standards
* Implementations utilizing WSC Core tracking tech must maintain first-party data sovereignty.
* Tracking solutions must eliminate reliance on third-party cookies and comply fully with regional privacy regulations (such as GDPR and CCPA).

---

## §3 Change Management & Operational Integrity

### 3.1 Transparent Performance Reporting
* Client reporting must explicitly differentiate between organic search performance, paid media, referral pathways, and automated AI/bot traffic streams[cite: 1].
* Performance dips resulting from search algorithm updates or technical releases must be documented and communicated transparently.

3.2 Safety & Deployment Protocols
* Technical modifications, canonical shifts, or domain-wide redirect structures must undergo verification in a staging environment prior to production deployment.
* Agencies must maintain rollback capability and operational backups for all client infrastructure under active management.

---

## §4 Governance & Dispute Resolution

### 4.1 Referral to the WSC Arbitration Board
In the event of operational disputes, unfulfilled handoff requirements, or alleged violations of this Code between clients and certified members, either party may file a petition with the **WSC Arbitration Board**.

### 4.2 Arbitration & Enforcement Mechanisms
* **Independent Audit:** The WSC Arbitration Board conducts an objective evaluation of project repositories, communication logs, and WTP Exit Package compliance.
* **Binding Resolution:** Findings may require mandatory technical remediation, partial fee restitution, or administrative suspension/revocation of WSC Certified Partner status and public directory listing.
