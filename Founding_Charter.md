# World Search Council (WSC) — Founding Charter

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Official Draft  
**Governance Body:** World Search Council (WSC)  
**Maintained by:** WSC Steering Committee & Founding Contributors  

---

## Preamble & Mission

The **World Search Council (WSC)** is established as a global, decentralized organization dedicated to standardizing, protecting, and advancing digital search, Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), and web experience engineering.

The Council operates on three foundational pillars:
1. **Standardization & Ethics:** Defining and maintaining accountable, engineering-grade industry standards.
2. **Governance & Arbitration:** Enforcing rules and resolving disputes through an independent internal board.
3. **Shared Technology ("OpenStack" Model):** Providing open-source, privacy-first technical infrastructure and tools for members.

---

## Article I: The Three-Phase Roadmap to Independence

To ensure sustainable growth and true decentralization, the WSC follows a structured three-phase transition model[cite: 2]:

### Phase 1: Initiation
* **Initiator:** The WSC is initiated by **searchneedsLOVE** as the founding organization.
* **Responsibilities:** searchneedsLOVE provides initial cloud/hardware infrastructure, moderation, seed funding, and the initial draft charter[cite: 2].
* **Governance:** Operational setup and initial standard drafting are led by the founding engineering team.

### Phase 2: Regional Representation
* **National Councils:** Regional and national WSC chapters are formed across major global markets.
* **Assembly Delegation:** Each national chapter elects 1–2 representatives to join the global WSC Assembly.
* **Democratic Transition:** Strategic decisions and standard ratifications transition to democratic assembly voting.

### Phase 3: Total Independence
* **Legal Entity:** The WSC transitions into a fully independent, non-profit legal entity (e.g., Foundation or International Association).
* **Founding Contributor Role:** searchneedsLOVE transitions permanently to a **Founding Contributor**, relinquishing operational control to the elected Assembly.

---

## Article II: Vendor Neutrality Clause

No software vendor, analytics platform, agency, cloud provider, or commercial entity shall hold preferential status, exclusive access, or permanent veto power within the World Search Council. 

* All standards, APIs, and open-source tools maintained by the WSC must remain fully interoperable and vendor-agnostic.
* Technical recommendations must be based solely on objective performance, user privacy, and engineering validity.

---

## Article III: Membership, Governance & Structure

### 3.1 Separation of Powers
The WSC strictly separates operational execution, technical maintenance, and legislative governance:
* **WSC Assembly:** The supreme decision-making body composed of elected regional representatives.
* **Working Groups:** Open-source engineering and policy teams responsible for maintaining RFCs, codebase repositories, and documentation.
* **Arbitration Board:** Independent dispute resolution body handling compliance, ethics violations, and audit enforcement.

### 3.2 No Permanent Founder Privilege
* There is no permanent "founder override" or permanent governing board seat for any individual or founding corporation.
* Governance rights are derived strictly from active participation, peer election, and democratic consensus.

---

## Article IV: The First Five Global Standards

The WSC commits to establishing and maintaining open technical specifications across five critical web pillars:

1. **First-Party Analytics & Data Sovereignty:** Standards for cookieless, GDPR-compliant server-side telemetry and event processing.
2. **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & RAG Indexing:** Protocols for entity clarity, semantic JSON-LD structures, and AI agent discovery (`/llms.txt`).
3. **Bot Defense & Crawler Governance:** Fair-use crawling thresholds, rate-limiting directives, and bandwidth protection standards.
4. **Anti-Spam & Synthetic Content Abuse:** Rules preventing programmatic garbage publishing, hallucination farming, and domain manipulation.
5. **Transparency & Audit Integrity:** Standardized reporting guidelines and exit handoff protocols via the WSC Transfer Protocol (WTP).
