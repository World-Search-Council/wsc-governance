# World Search Council (WSC) — Code of Search Ethics

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Official Draft  
**Governance Body:** World Search Council (WSC)  
**Maintained by:** WSC Open Standards & Governance Working Group  

---

## Preamble

The **World Search Council (WSC)** is dedicated to advancing Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Search Experience Engineering, and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) into an accountable, transparent, and engineering-grade discipline. 

As digital search transitions from traditional keyword-indexed pages to artificial intelligence, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and vector spaces, the responsibility of search practitioners toward web users, clients, and the broader information ecosystem has never been greater.

This **Code of Search Ethics** defines the foundational, non-negotiable principles required of all WSC members, certified agencies, independent consultants, and contributing partners. Adherence to this Code serves as a binding mandate for WSC certification and ecosystem participation.

---

## The Four Fundamental Pillars

### Pillar I: Transparency & Truthfulness in Reporting

1.1 **Honest Performance Representation**  
* Practitioners shall not fabricate, manipulate, or misrepresent traffic metrics, search rankings, or conversion data.
* All client reporting must clearly distinguish between organic search traffic, paid acquisition, referral paths, and bot/AI crawler activity.

1.2 **No Synthetic Score Gamification**  
* Practitioners must not optimize exclusively for artificial third-party metrics or checklist-driven audit scores at the expense of real-world site health, user experience, and search utility.

1.3 **Full Conflict of Interest Disclosure**  
* Any financial affiliations, sponsored content relationships, affiliate models, or proprietary tool preferences that impact strategic recommendations must be disclosed to clients prior to contract execution.

---

### Pillar II: Data Privacy, Security & Technical Integrity

2.1 **First-Party Privacy & Data Sovereignty**  
* Practitioners must adhere to global privacy frameworks (including GDPR, CCPA, and regional data protection laws).
* Tracking solutions and analytics telemetry must prioritize first-party data architecture, minimizing invasive user tracking and avoiding unsanctioned data sharing with unverified third parties.

2.2 **Zero Malicious Manipulation**  
* Cloaking, malicious redirects, hidden text, predatory doorway pages, and destructive parasite SEO tactics are strictly prohibited under WSC standards.
* Security headers, SSL/TLS protocols, and domain governance must be maintained to safeguard end-users against malware, phishing, or hijacked domain authority.

2.3 **Respect for Web Infrastructure & Crawling Governance**  
* Crawling, scraping, and AI model ingestion practices must respect `robots.txt`, `/llms.txt`, and server capacity constraints, avoiding intentional denial-of-service or bandwidth extraction patterns.

---

### Pillar III: Intent-Driven, Sustainable & Quality Optimization

3.1 **User-Centric & Intent-Space Alignment**  
* Optimization strategies must focus on solving real user intent (Need-Deliberation-Action) rather than exploiting temporary algorithm gaps or generating zero-value programmatic spam.

3.2 **Responsible AI & Content Generation**  
* AI-generated content and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) workflows must meet standards of factual accuracy, information density, and entity clarity.
* Hallucination farming, automated misrepresentation, and mass-scale automated garbage publishing (scaled content abuse) are explicitly forbidden.

3.3 **Entity & Brand Authenticity**  
* Schema markup, structured JSON-LD data, and Knowledge Graph representations must reflect accurate, verifiable entity data without misleading claims of affiliation or expertise.

---

### Pillar IV: Client Sovereignty & Interoperability

4.1 **Anti-Lock-In Commitment**  
* Practitioners shall never hold a client's digital assets, domain credentials, analytics telemetry, or search infrastructure hostage.

4.2 **Adherence to the WSC Transfer Protocol (WTP)**  
* Upon termination of an engagement, practitioners are required to deliver a standardized WTP Exit Package containing complete technical documentation, active redirects, schema mappings, and historical audit logs.

4.3 **Open Standards & Tool Neutrality**  
* Recommendations must prioritize client success over vendor kickbacks. Practitioners must support open, interoperable formats and standards defined by the WSC.

---

## Compliance & Dispute Resolution

Violation of this Code of Search Ethics compromises the integrity of the WSC ecosystem and may result in formal review by the **WSC Arbitration Board**.

### Enforcement Actions
1. **Formal Audit & Notice:** Investigation of reported code violations.
2. **Mandatory Remediation:** Required corrective action within a specified timeline.
3. **Certification Suspension / Revocation:** Loss of WSC Certified Partner status, badge removal, and public registry status updating (`worldsearchcouncil.org/verify/[ID]`).

## Governance & Amendments
